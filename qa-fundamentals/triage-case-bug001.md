# Triage case

**bug-001**: IRIE20 discount does not apply when cart total is exactly 10,000 JMD

## What happened?
The IRIE20 discount is not being applied when the cart total is exactly 10,000 JMD.

## Who is affected? 
Customers who have a cart total of exactly 10,000 JMD and try to use the IRIE20 discount.

## How badly does it break the promise? 
It breaks the promise of providing a discount to eligible customers, leading to a negative user experience.

## Why should the team act now, or why can it wait? 
The issue should be addressed as soon as possible to ensure that eligible customers receive the discount they are entitled to.

## summary - 30 seconds
The IRIE20 discount is not being applied when the cart total is exactly 10,000 JMD, which affects customers who are eligible for the discount. This issue should be addressed as soon as possible to ensure that eligible customers receive the discount they are entitled to.
