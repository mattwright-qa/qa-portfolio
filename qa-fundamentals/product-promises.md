# Product promises and risks

- IRIE20 gives 20% off orders of 10,000 JMD or more.

- Delivery costs 500 JMD, free on orders of 15,000 JMD or more (before discount). Pickup is always free.

- You can order up to 10 of any one dish.

- A sold out dish cannot be added to an order.

- Passwords are 8 to 64 characters. 

- The cart badge shows a count, so the count should always match what is in the order.

- Card payments need a 16-digit number, a future or current-month expiry (MM/YY), and a 3-digit CVV.

- Your order, favourites and ratings survive a refresh. Nothing leaves your browser.

# USER STORY

## As a shopper, I want to sign in to my Tumble Kitchen account so I can reorder my usual dishes.

# Criteria and Test Ideas

- Sign-in requires a valid email format : test with invalid email format and verify clear error message is shown and does not sign in the shopper.

- Password must be 8 to 64 characters : test with 7 characters.

- A wrong email or password shows a clear error and does not reveal which one was wrong : test that error message explains the problem without revealing which field was wrong after a failed sign-in.

- A signed-in shopper sees their name and can reorder : test that the name is shown after a successful sign-in.

# Missing criteria

What if the shopper has no account?

What if the shopper has an account but has never ordered before?

What if the shopper has an account but has no usual dishes? all orders are one-offs?
